# Content selector: frozen analysis protocol

Registered before computing selector validation results, 2026-09-09.

- Analysis checkpoint: existing seed17 Hard Patch16/stride8/Top10, continuation epoch 20. No checkpoint selection on validation; this is the user-specified incumbent.
- Use all 50 local DIV2K validation images, independently of the 50 formal test images. Training images and test images are excluded from selector design. Verify split identity and content hashes first.
- One fixed 128×128 view per image: resize RGB to 140×140 (PIL bilinear), center crop [6:134,6:134], normalize to [-1,1]. Generate 64-bit messages with a dedicated CPU Torch generator, seed 170901. Persist tensors, source identities, image hashes and messages before encoding.
- Predetermined qualitative examples: sorted validation indices 0, 10, 20, 30, 40. No replacements based on visual appearance or scores.
- Exactly seven rows: MSE baseline, gradient-only and equal gradient+HF at alpha 0.5, 1, 2. Patch16, stride8, 225 patches; ceil(0.10×225)=23 and ceil(0.25×225)=57 selected patches. Stable descending sort with original row-major patch index as tie-break.
- Features use detached original RGB transformed to [0,1], luminance 0.299R+0.587G+0.114B. On each patch, G is the mean of sqrt(dx²+dy²) using paired forward differences on its upper-left 15×15 sites. H is mean squared response to the 4-neighbor Laplacian on the inner 14×14 sites. This measures local high-frequency energy without image-padding edges. Variance is population luminance variance. Edge density is fraction of the same gradient sites exceeding the per-image 75th percentile of all patch gradient sites (diagnostic only).
- Independently normalize G and H within each image: clamp((x−q05)/(q95−q05),0,1); when q95−q05 <= 1e−12 set normalized activity to zero. Gradient-only A=Ghat; combined A=(Ghat+Hhat)/2; score=MSE/(1+alpha*A). Scores and activity are detached. If subsequently authorized by the gate, the optimized local loss remains the arithmetic mean of ORIGINAL MSE at selected indices.
- LPIPS 0.1 AlexNet cannot take native 16×16 crops. Primary patch LPIPS is explicitly a proxy: bilinearly resize both 16×16 RGB crops to 32×32, align_corners=False, no clipping of encoded tensors. This changes sampling scale and is not identical to the historical native-32 metric or a human artifact judgment. Supplemental contextual LPIPS: run official spatial=True on whole 128×128 image pairs and mean-pool its output on the identical patch16/stride8 grid. It is not used to switch candidate rankings after results.
- SSIM degradation: 1−SSIM on native RGB 16×16 patches, existing Gaussian 5×5, sigma=1.5, data_range=2 implementation. The shared implementation uses zero padding. No selection based on changing perceptual metric definitions.
- Average per-image Spearman and patch-index Jaccard. Images, not overlapping patches, are the independent sampling units. Record selected raw G/H/variance/edge density, selected activity percentiles, and fraction of selected patches in the lowest activity quintile.
- Uncertainty: paired image bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, fixed NumPy seed 170902. Report percentile 95% confidence intervals (descriptive, unadjusted for six-candidate comparison).
- Training gate (operational definition of clear improvement): either primary LPIPS or SSIM Spearman improves by >=0.03 and its paired CI lower bound >0, AND Top10 Jaccard for that same target improves by >=0.02 and its paired CI lower bound >0. Reject a candidate if the other target's Spearman falls by >0.03 or its Top10 Jaccard falls by >0.02. Reject degenerate flat collapse if mean lowest-activity-quintile selected fraction exceeds 0.60, or mean selected activity percentile falls below 0.25. Require selected mean gradient to decrease relative to baseline as evidence of reduced texture bias.
- Selection among gate-passing candidates first prefers improvements for both targets, then larger sum of the two Spearman changes. Treat candidates within 0.01 of that summed change as comparable; prefer gradient-only, then lower alpha. If none pass, report the best descriptive candidate by the same ordering, but select NO training formulation and terminate normalization tuning. Thresholds are engineering screening criteria, not a confirmatory statistical claim.
- No training or formal test evaluation in the analysis script. Freeze exactly one formulation in the decision artifact before any conditional training. A successful screening result is not evidence of test improvement. No additional ratios, alpha values, feature weights or seeds.

LPIPS implementation reference: [official source](https://github.com/richzhang/PerceptualSimilarity/blob/master/lpips/lpips.py).
