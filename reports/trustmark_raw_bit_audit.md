# TrustMark raw-bit audit

No model was trained. This audit uses the official TrustMark Q/P decoder and the same fixed formal crop masks used by the MBRS evaluator.

## Verified pre-ECC path

The official `trustmark.py::subimage_decode` implementation performs:

1. official image preprocessing and resize to the model decoder resolution;
2. `secret_logits = tm.decoder.decoder(stego)`;
3. `secret_binaryarray = (secret_logits > 0)`;
4. `DataLayer.decode_bitstream(secret_binaryarray, MODE)` for BCH/ECC correction and message validation.

Therefore the following are available without changing the model or strength:

- **A — raw/pre-ECC bits:** the official 100-bit thresholded decoder packet;
- **protected bits:** the expected 100-bit BCH-5 packet generated by the official `DataLayer.encode_binary`;
- **decoder logits:** the official decoder output before thresholding;
- **B — ECC-corrected success:** official `DataLayer` result matching the expected 61-bit message;
- **C — detection/decode success:** official `detected=True` result.

Raw bit accuracy is computed against the protected 100-bit packet, not against the 61-bit data payload. Sigmoid probabilities were not used for BER because the official pipeline exposes logits and defines bits by `logit > 0`; logits and absolute-logit summaries are retained in `trustmark_raw_bits/per_trial.csv`.

## Interpretation

TrustMark Q has better raw-bit and ECC robustness than P at every partial crop. P has better image quality but a worse robustness trade-off. Raw BER and ECC exact success are intentionally reported separately: at 50% crop, Q has raw accuracy `0.948080` but exact success `0.5840`, because detection and BCH correction are stricter than individual protected-bit accuracy.

This result is available for analysis, but TrustMark remains a `REFERENCE` comparison: it uses 100 protected bits with BCH-5 and 61 data bits, while MBRS reports raw 64-bit BER without ECC.
