## Formal evidence contract

- Main table: eight existing seed17 epoch20 checkpoints, common source and current clipped-RGB evaluator.
- Formal local grid: native32 non-overlap, 16 patches/image; Top25 uses 4 patches and local Top10 uses 2 patches.
- Training grid: patch16, stride8, 225 candidates/image; Top10 selects 23 candidates.
- Quality PSNR: dataset mean clipped-RGB MSE. Local PSNR: mean of per-image tail PSNR values. P95: mean of per-image P95 values.
- Robustness: raw 64-bit BER on fixed normalized tensor masks, with five repeats; no claim about saved-image deployment.
