## A. Frozen method and baseline definitions

Frozen final method:

```text
Ours = Hard Local-Tail + global OKLab
L = 10 L_msg + 0.5 L_RGB + 0.5 L_tail + 0.059149764 L_OKLab
```

Frozen details: P16, stride 8, hard Top-10%, 225 overlapping candidates, 23 selected patches, `RandomCrop(0.3, 1.0)`, 20 continuation epochs, `lr = 1e-4`, batch size 16, seed17, restored Adam and BatchNorm/model state, and no additional inference module.

Internal baseline: MBRS crop-trained global, using the same MBRS backbone and the global RGB reconstruction objective without the local-tail term. Hard Local-Tail is the intermediate ablation. External methods are contextual Figure 2 references only.

The frozen seed17 source and final checkpoint identities are taken from the bundled frozen-method evidence; no new checkpoint may replace them in the paper.

## B. New experiments and analyses

### Priority 1: paired image-level bootstrap

- Reuse the existing frozen per-image project-test output explicitly referenced by the authoritative bundle; do not rerun training for this phase.
- Use the same 50 project-test images and preserve method pairing.
- Comparisons: Global vs Ours; Global vs Hard; Hard vs Ours when all rows are available.
- Use `20,000` bootstrap resamples of image indices with replacement.
- Bootstrap analysis seed: `20260916`.
- Compute mean-difference bootstrap distributions and 95% percentile intervals for PSNR, Top-25 Local PSNR, P95 MSE, P99 MSE, LPIPS, Global CIEDE2000, Top10 CIEDE2000, and Gini.
- For BER, resample image-level BER contributions, not individual bits; report BER100, BER70, BER50, BER40, and BER30.
- Report observed difference, 2.5 percentile, 97.5 percentile, bootstrap standard error, and whether the interval crosses zero.
- Difference convention: `second method − first method`; higher-is-better metrics are favorable when positive, lower-is-better metrics are favorable when negative.
- Use “the paired bootstrap interval excludes/includes zero”; do not use “statistically significant” unless directly justified by the pre-registered interval procedure.

## C. Additional random seeds

The pre-registered seed set is `17, 23, 42`. Seed17 is the frozen paper seed. Seeds23 and42 are fixed in advance and must not be selected, dropped, or replaced based on results. The source-checkpoint availability gate in Priority 4 is mandatory.

## D. Metrics and comparison conventions

Primary image-level metrics: PSNR, Top-25 Local PSNR, P95 patch MSE, P99 patch MSE, LPIPS, Global CIEDE2000, Top10 CIEDE2000, Gini, and BER100/70/50/40/30.

All paired analyses preserve image identity. Lower is better for P95, P99, LPIPS, CIEDE2000, Gini, and BER; higher is better for PSNR and local PSNR. Gini remains a diagnostic and is never promoted to the primary objective.

The three metric spaces remain separate: natural frozen 128×128 project-test, internal matched-PSNR 128×128 project-test, and external Figure 2 matched display-PSNR on the common 512×512 validation canvas. External BER is not strictly comparable and is not ranked.

## E. Validation/project-test usage

- Validation may be used to freeze the alpha grid, calibrate operating points, and check protocol behavior.
- Project-test may be evaluated only after all choices affecting a phase are frozen.
- No project-test result may select a hyperparameter, checkpoint, seed, alpha point, sample, ROI, or final method.
- The frozen seed17 paper numbers remain unchanged even if extension results differ.

## F. Stopping rules

- Stop a phase when its pre-registered output and report are complete.
- Do not rerun a valid frozen seed17 analysis unnecessarily.
- Stop and document any phase whose exact protocol cannot be implemented without guessing or changing the design.
- Stop the multi-seed phase if exact seed-specific source checkpoints for seeds23/42 are unavailable.
- Stop training branches after the specified 20 continuation epochs; no early stopping or outcome-based extension.
- Do not create a new final method or retune any frozen parameter.
- Negative, mixed, null, and failed checks must remain in the reports.

## G. No-retuning policy

Do not retune `lambda_OK`, Top-k fraction, patch size, stride, message weight, continuation length, source checkpoint policy, or any other frozen method choice using new project-test results. No new branch may be promoted automatically.
